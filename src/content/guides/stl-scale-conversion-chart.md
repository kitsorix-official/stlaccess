## How to Use This Chart

1. Find your **source scale** in the left column
2. Find your **target scale** across the top
3. Where they intersect is the **exact percentage** to type into your slicer
4. Lock the aspect ratio in Chitubox, Lychee, or PrusaSlicer
5. Enter the percentage in any scale field — the other axes update automatically

## Important Notes

- **Gaming scales** (28mm, 32mm, etc.) measure to eye level of a 175cm human figure
- **Ratio scales** (1:48, 1:56, etc.) measure to the top of the head
- Add **1-3% for resin shrinkage** if printing with UV-curable resin
- These percentages assume the original STL was modelled at the stated scale — if the file was originally designed for a different scale, adjust accordingly
