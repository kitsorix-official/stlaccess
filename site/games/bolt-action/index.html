3D Printing Tips for Bolt Action

28mm infantry prints well on FDM. 1:56 vehicles (tanks, jeeps, trucks) are the FDM sweet spot for detail and speed.

FDM rarely needs compensation; slight over-extrusion usually cancels any cooling shrinkage.
