3D Printing Tips for Gaslands

1:56 is the FDM sweet spot for weapons, armor, and accessories for toy car conversions. Print weapon mounts, roll cages, and armor plates.

1:56 tanks are the sweet spot for FDM: large enough for detail, small enough to print quickly.
