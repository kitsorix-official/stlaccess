How to Use the Scale Converter

  1. 1

    Select Your Source Scale

    Choose the scale of your original miniature from the dropdown. Supports 15+ scales including 28mm, 32mm, 35mm, 40mm, 75mm, 1:35, 1:48, 1:56, 1:72, and 1:100.

  2. 2

    Select Your Target Scale

    Choose the scale you want to convert to. The converter calculates the exact percentage using eye-level measurements from a 175cm human baseline (160cm eye level).

  3. 3

    Apply the Percentage

    Set your slicer to the calculated percentage using uniform scaling across all axes. For example, 114.3% converts 28mm miniatures to 32mm scale for Warhammer 40,000 Primaris.

Why I Built STLACCESS

I'm kitsorix, a 3D printing hobbyist who got tired of Googling "what percentage to scale 28mm to 32mm" every time I converted miniatures between systems. STLACCESS is a free, browser-based tool that does the math for you — no downloads, no accounts, no ads. The conversion data is verified against community sources.

The eye-level measurement baseline uses 160cm on a 175cm human, which matches the community convention for gaming scales. Ratio scales (1:35, 1:48, 1:56, 1:72, 1:100) use top-of-head measurements at 175cm, matching the standard for scale modeling.
