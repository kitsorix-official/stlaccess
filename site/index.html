How to Use

  1. 1

    Pick your scales

    Choose your source miniature scale (what you currently have) and your target scale (what you need). The converter supports 28mm, 32mm, 35mm, 40mm, 75mm, 1:35, 1:48, 1:56, 1:72, and 1:100.

  2. 2

    Read the percentage

    The scale converter calculates the exact percentage using eye-level measurements from a 175cm human baseline (160cm eye level for gaming scales, top-of-head for ratio scales).

  3. 3

    Set your slicer

    Enter the percentage as uniform scaling across all axes in Chitubox, Lychee, or Cura. For example, 114.3% converts 28mm miniatures to 32mm scale. Print, measure, verify.

Frequently Asked Questions

What percentage do I scale 28mm to 32mm?

114.3%. Set your slicer to 114.3% uniform scaling when converting 28mm miniatures (D&D, Bolt Action) to 32mm (Warhammer 40K, Age of Sigmar). Calculated as 32mm ÷ 28mm = 1.143.

What scale are D&D miniatures?

28mm scale (1:56 ratio). D&D Medium humanoids are 28mm to eye level. Large creatures are ~56mm, Huge are ~112mm, Gargantuan are ~224mm.

How do I change 32mm to 28mm?

87.5%. Set your slicer to 87.5% uniform scaling to downscale 32mm miniatures to 28mm. Calculated as 28mm ÷ 32mm = 0.875.

How do I scale an STL file?

Open your STL in Chitubox or Lychee, select the model, go to Scale, and enter the percentage with uniform scaling enabled. For 28mm to 32mm, enter 114.3%. Measure the result with calipers to verify.

What is 1:56 scale in mm?

28mm to the eye level (31.3mm to top of head) on a 175cm human baseline. 1:56 is the standard ratio for 28mm wargaming — Bolt Action, D&D, Frostgrave.

How do I scale an STL file online?

Use STLACCESS — a free browser-based STL scale converter. Select your source and target scales, read the percentage, and enter it in your slicer (Chitubox, Lychee, Cura) with uniform scaling enabled. No downloads or accounts required.

What is the STL scale conversion chart for 32mm miniatures?

The most common 32mm conversions: 28mm to 32mm = 114.3%, 32mm to 28mm = 87.5%, 35mm to 32mm = 91.4%, 1:56 to 32mm = 111.9%. Use the conversion chart above or the calculator for exact percentages.
