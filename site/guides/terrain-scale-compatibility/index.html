Why Terrain Scale Matters

Getting terrain scale right is just as important as getting miniature scale right. A doorway that's too small for your minis breaks immersion. A building that's too large dwarfs your army. Terrain scale determines how your miniatures interact with the battlefield.

Terrain Scale by Miniature Scale

28mm miniatures (D&D, Bolt Action, Frostgrave): Use 1:56 terrain. Standard doorways should be 35-37mm tall. Walls should be 40-50mm tall. This matches the 28mm eye-level standard.

32mm miniatures (40k, AoS, Kill Team): Use 1:48 terrain. Standard doorways should be 40-45mm tall. Walls should be 50-60mm tall. Slightly larger than 28mm terrain to match the heroic proportions.

35mm miniatures (Legion, Conquest): Use 1:48 terrain. Standard doorways should be 45-50mm tall. Walls should be 55-65mm tall. Close to 1:48 scale model buildings.

40mm miniatures (Shatterpoint, MCP): Use 1:43 terrain. Standard doorways should be 50-55mm tall. Walls should be 60-70mm tall. Larger scale to match the character-focused gameplay.

Universal Terrain Rule

For any scale, the doorway rule works: a standard door (200cm real) should be taller than your miniatures' total height (top of head + base). If your 32mm minis fit through a 45mm doorway, the scale is correct.

3D Printing Terrain Tips

Print terrain at 0.2mm layer height with a 0.4mm nozzle for speed. Terrain doesn't need the same detail as minis. Use 15-20% infill for buildings to save weight and material. Orient walls vertically for best surface finish.
