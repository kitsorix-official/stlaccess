Bolt Action Scale Overview

Bolt Action uses 28mm scale for infantry and 1:56 scale for vehicles. This combination provides realistic proportions on the tabletop. Infantry measure 28mm to eye level, and a Sherman tank at 1:56 is approximately 14cm long.

Vehicle Scale Compatibility

1:56 is the standard vehicle scale for Bolt Action. 1:48 vehicles work but are slightly oversized. 1:35 vehicles are too large for 28mm infantry but make excellent display pieces.

3D Printing for Bolt Action

FDM is ideal for Bolt Action because the 28mm scale and 1:56 vehicles fall in the sweet spot for FDM detail. Use 0.2mm nozzle at 0.06mm layer height for infantry, and 0.4mm nozzle at 0.2mm for vehicles.
