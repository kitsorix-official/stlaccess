FDM Settings for Miniatures

Use a 0.2mm nozzle for miniatures under 40mm. Layer height should be 0.06-0.08mm for faces and detail areas. Print speed should be 20-30mm/s for outer walls. Use tree supports for overhangs.

Orientation Matters

Print miniatures face-up whenever possible. This puts the most visible detail (face, chest) on the top surface where FDM has the best quality. Avoid printing faces against the build plate.

Post-Processing

Light sanding with 400-600 grit removes minor layer lines. A coat of filler primer (such as Tamiya Surface Primer) fills remaining lines before painting. Two thin coats of primer are better than one thick coat.
