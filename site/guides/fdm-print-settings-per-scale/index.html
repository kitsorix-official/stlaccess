FDM Print Settings Per Scale — Layer Height, Nozzle, and Speed Guide

Why Print Settings Vary by Scale

Smaller miniatures need finer settings to capture detail. Larger miniatures can use coarser settings for speed. The wrong settings waste time or produce poor results. Match your settings to the scale for best results.

6mm-10mm Scale (Epic)

Nozzle: 0.2mm (required for detail). Layer height: 0.04-0.06mm. Speed: 15-20mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 20-30%. Notes: 6mm infantry is nearly impossible on FDM — use resin. 6mm-10mm vehicles and mechs are manageable with a 0.2mm nozzle.

15mm Scale (Historical)

Nozzle: 0.2mm recommended. Layer height: 0.06-0.08mm. Speed: 20-25mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 15-20%. Notes: 15mm infantry benefits from resin. Vehicles and terrain print well on FDM with a 0.2mm nozzle.

28mm Scale (D&D, Bolt Action)

Nozzle: 0.2mm for infantry, 0.4mm for terrain. Layer height: 0.06-0.08mm. Speed: 20-30mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 15-20%. Notes: The FDM sweet spot. 28mm miniatures print well with basic FDM settings. Resin gives cleaner facial detail.

32mm Scale (40k, AoS)

Nozzle: 0.2mm for infantry, 0.4mm for vehicles. Layer height: 0.06-0.08mm. Speed: 20-30mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 15-20%. Notes: 32mm is forgiving on FDM. Layer lines are less visible on larger surfaces. Heroic proportions hide imperfections.

35mm-40mm Scale (Legion, MCP)

Nozzle: 0.2mm for detail, 0.4mm for speed. Layer height: 0.06-0.08mm. Speed: 20-30mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 15-20%. Notes: Large enough for FDM to handle facial detail well. Use 0.2mm nozzle at 0.06mm layer height for faces.

75mm Scale (Display)

Nozzle: 0.2mm required. Layer height: 0.04-0.06mm. Speed: 15-20mm/s for outer walls. Infill: 10-15%. Notes: 75mm on FDM needs very fine settings. Slow print speeds are essential for acceptable surface quality. Consider resin for the best results.
