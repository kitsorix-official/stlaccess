Converting Real-World Objects to 32mm Scale

Formula and reference chart for converting real-world measurements to 32mm miniature scale for terrain and scratch-building.

The Formula

To convert any real-world measurement to 32mm scale: (inches × 25.4 / 72) × 32 = mm at 32mm scale. Simplified: inches × 11.29 = mm. For metric: cm × 1.829 = mm at 32mm scale.

Quick Reference

A 6-foot (183cm) human = 32mm. A standard door (6'8" / 203cm) = 35.7mm. A car (15ft / 457cm) = 203mm. A two-story house (20ft / 610cm) = 271mm.

Application for Terrain

When building scratch terrain, measure the real object, apply the formula, and cut materials to the calculated miniature size. This ensures buildings, walls, and accessories look proportional next to 32mm miniatures.
