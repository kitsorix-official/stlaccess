1:48 to 28mm Scale Conversion

Scale 1:48 miniatures to <strong>76.8%</strong> when converting to 28mm for 3D printed STLs.

The Math Behind 76.8%

To convert 1:48 miniatures to 28mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

28.0mm ÷ 33.3mm = 76.8%

This means if your 1:48 miniature is 33.3mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 28mm should be approximately 28.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 76.8% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 76.8% when converting 1:48 miniatures (such as US O-gauge trains, Bandai Star Wars kits, Aircraft model kits) to 28mm scale (such as Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Bolt Action infantry, Mantic Kings of War, Frostgrave, Infinity, Kill Team, Necromunda). Common for vehicles and aircraft. Figures are usually accessories to vehicle kits.

Source Scale: 1:48

1:48 miniatures measure 33.3mm to the eye level (36.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:48 scale. Common for vehicles and aircraft. Figures are usually accessories to vehicle kits.

3D Printing: 1:48 aircraft wingspans can be large; plan bed orientation carefully.

Common Issue: 1:48 figures are often molded in soft vinyl rather than hard plastic.

Target Scale: 28mm

28mm miniatures measure 28.0mm to the eye level (30.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:56 scale. The classic tabletop standard. Measures to the eyes (160cm on a 175cm human), not the top of the head.

3D Printing: FDM rarely needs compensation; slight over-extrusion usually cancels any cooling shrinkage.

Common Issue: Older sculpts may be closer to 25mm true-scale. Always check the sculptor's baseline before scaling.
