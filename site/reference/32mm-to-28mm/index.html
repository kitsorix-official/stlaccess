The Math Behind 87.5%

To convert 32mm miniatures to 28mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

28.0mm ÷ 32.0mm = 87.5%

This means if your 32mm miniature is 32.0mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 28mm should be approximately 28.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 87.5% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 87.5% when converting 32mm miniatures (such as Warhammer 40,000 (Primaris), Age of Sigmar, Malifaux, Fallout: Wasteland Warfare, Grimdark Future, Kill Team, Necromunda, Warcry) to 28mm scale (such as Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Bolt Action infantry, Mantic Kings of War, Frostgrave, Infinity, Kill Team, Necromunda). Games Workshop shifted from 28mm to 32mm between 2014 and 2015 for more facial detail and heroic proportions.

Source Scale: 32mm

32mm miniatures measure 32.0mm to the eye level (34.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:50 scale. Games Workshop shifted from 28mm to 32mm between 2014 and 2015 for more facial detail and heroic proportions.

3D Printing: 32mm is forgiving on FDM; layer lines are less visible on larger surfaces.

Common Issue: Some third-party sculptors label 35mm models as '32mm heroic.' Check eye-level measurement, not total height.

Target Scale: 28mm

28mm miniatures measure 28.0mm to the eye level (30.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:56 scale. The classic tabletop standard. Measures to the eyes (160cm on a 175cm human), not the top of the head.

3D Printing: FDM rarely needs compensation; slight over-extrusion usually cancels any cooling shrinkage.

Common Issue: Older sculpts may be closer to 25mm true-scale. Always check the sculptor's baseline before scaling.

Reverse Conversion

To convert in the other direction, see 32mm to 28mm — 114.3%.
