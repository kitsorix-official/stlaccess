The Math Behind 109.4%

To convert 32mm miniatures to 35mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

35.0mm ÷ 32.0mm = 109.4%

This means if your 32mm miniature is 32.0mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 35mm should be approximately 35.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 109.4% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 109.4% when converting 32mm miniatures (such as Warhammer 40,000 (Primaris), Age of Sigmar, Malifaux, Fallout: Wasteland Warfare, Grimdark Future, Kill Team, Necromunda, Warcry) to 35mm scale (such as Star Wars: Legion, Kingdom Death). Games Workshop shifted from 28mm to 32mm between 2014 and 2015 for more facial detail and heroic proportions.

Source Scale: 32mm

32mm miniatures measure 32.0mm to the eye level (34.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:50 scale. Games Workshop shifted from 28mm to 32mm between 2014 and 2015 for more facial detail and heroic proportions.

3D Printing: 32mm is forgiving on FDM; layer lines are less visible on larger surfaces.

Common Issue: Some third-party sculptors label 35mm models as '32mm heroic.' Check eye-level measurement, not total height.

Target Scale: 35mm

35mm miniatures measure 35.0mm to the eye level (38.0mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:48 scale. Popular with painters who want more canvas for freehand detail without entering true bust territory.

3D Printing: FDM at 35mm benefits from a 0.2mm nozzle at 0.06mm layer height for faces.

Common Issue: 35mm is sometimes marketed as 'true 35mm' (realistic proportions) vs 'heroic 35mm' (exaggerated). Check before mixing.

Reverse Conversion

To convert in the other direction, see 32mm to 35mm — 91.4%.
