28mm to 40mm Scale Conversion

Scale 28mm miniatures to <strong>142.9%</strong> when converting to 40mm for 3D printed STLs.

The Math Behind 142.9%

To convert 28mm miniatures to 40mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

40.0mm ÷ 28.0mm = 142.9%

This means if your 28mm miniature is 28.0mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 40mm should be approximately 40.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 142.9% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 142.9% when converting 28mm miniatures (such as Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Bolt Action infantry, Mantic Kings of War, Frostgrave, Infinity, Kill Team, Necromunda) to 40mm scale (such as Star Wars: Shatterpoint, Marvel: Crisis Protocol, Inquisitor (GW), Display vignettes). The classic tabletop standard. Measures to the eyes (160cm on a 175cm human), not the top of the head.

Source Scale: 28mm

28mm miniatures measure 28.0mm to the eye level (30.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:56 scale. The classic tabletop standard. Measures to the eyes (160cm on a 175cm human), not the top of the head.

3D Printing: FDM rarely needs compensation; slight over-extrusion usually cancels any cooling shrinkage.

Common Issue: Older sculpts may be closer to 25mm true-scale. Always check the sculptor's baseline before scaling.

Target Scale: 40mm

40mm miniatures measure 40.0mm to the eye level (43.0mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:43 scale. Used for centerpiece models, display dioramas, or games where individual heroes need to stand out.

3D Printing: FDM works well at 40mm for monsters and vehicles with a 0.4mm nozzle.

Common Issue: 40mm bases can destabilize on uneven terrain. Consider magnetizing or weighting the base.
