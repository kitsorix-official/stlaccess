Scale 1:72 miniatures to <strong>308.6%</strong> when converting to 75mm for 3D printed STLs.

The Math Behind 308.6%

To convert 1:72 miniatures to 75mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

75.0mm ÷ 22.2mm = 308.6%

This means if your 1:72 miniature is 22.2mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 75mm should be approximately 75.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 308.6% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 308.6% when converting 1:72 miniatures (such as Airfix, Revell, Zvezda, Plastic Soldier Company) to 75mm scale (such as Display busts, Figure painting competitions, Scale 75 display line). Popular for large army builds on a budget. Figures are roughly 20-22mm to the eyes.

Source Scale: 1:72

1:72 miniatures measure 22.2mm to the eye level (24.3mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:72 scale. Popular for large army builds on a budget. Figures are roughly 20-22mm to the eyes.

3D Printing: 1:72 figures are challenging on FDM due to rifle barrels and bayonets. A 0.2mm nozzle is recommended.

Common Issue: 1:72 scale creep exists: modern sculpts are often closer to 1:64.

Target Scale: 75mm

75mm miniatures measure 75.0mm to the eye level (80.0mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:24 scale. Not a tabletop wargaming scale. Designed for contest judging and display cabinets.

3D Printing: 75mm on FDM needs a 0.2mm nozzle and very slow print speeds for acceptable surface quality.

Common Issue: 75mm sculpts often have separate heads or arms for easier painting access.
