1:100 to 28mm Scale Conversion

Scale 1:100 miniatures to <strong>160.0%</strong> when converting to 28mm for 3D printed STLs.

The Math Behind 160.0%

To convert 1:100 miniatures to 28mm, divide the target eye-level measurement by the source eye-level measurement:

28.0mm ÷ 16.0mm = 160.0%

This means if your 1:100 miniature is 16.0mm tall (eye level), the same figure at 28mm should be approximately 28.0mm tall. Set your slicer to 160.0% uniform scaling across all axes.

When to Use This Conversion

Use 160.0% when converting 1:100 miniatures (such as Flames of War, Team Yankee, 15mm wargaming, Micro-armor) to 28mm scale (such as Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder, Bolt Action infantry, Mantic Kings of War, Frostgrave, Infinity, Kill Team, Necromunda). Used for large tank battles and regiment-level games where table space is limited.

Source Scale: 1:100

1:100 miniatures measure 16.0mm to the eye level (17.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:100 scale. Used for large tank battles and regiment-level games where table space is limited.

3D Printing: 1:100 vehicles are manageable on FDM. Infantry at this scale is very challenging without a 0.2mm nozzle.

Common Issue: 1:100 and 15mm are often used interchangeably, but 15mm can mean 15mm to the eyes or 15mm total height.

Target Scale: 28mm

28mm miniatures measure 28.0mm to the eye level (30.5mm to the top of the head) on a 175cm human baseline. This is approximately 1:56 scale. The classic tabletop standard. Measures to the eyes (160cm on a 175cm human), not the top of the head.

3D Printing: FDM rarely needs compensation; slight over-extrusion usually cancels any cooling shrinkage.

Common Issue: Older sculpts may be closer to 25mm true-scale. Always check the sculptor's baseline before scaling.
