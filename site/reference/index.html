Reference Questions

Do you measure miniatures to the eyes or to the top of the head?

I measure to the eyes at 160cm on a 175cm human baseline. This matches the community convention for gaming scales (28mm, 32mm, 35mm, 40mm, 75mm). Ratio scales (1:35, 1:48, 1:56, 1:72, 1:100) use top-of-head measurements at 175cm, matching the standard for scale modeling.

What is the actual size difference between 28mm and 32mm miniatures?

28mm miniatures measure 28mm to the eye level (30.5mm to top of head). 32mm miniatures measure 32mm to the eye level (34.5mm to top of head). The difference is 4mm at eye level, which is about 14.3%. To convert between them, scale by 114.3% (28→32) or 87.5% (32→28).

What percentage do I scale 28mm to 32mm?

Set your slicer to 114.3% when converting 28mm miniatures to 32mm scale. This is calculated by dividing the target eye-level (32mm) by the source eye-level (28mm): 32 ÷ 28 = 1.143.

What percentage do I scale 32mm to 28mm?

Set your slicer to 87.5% when converting 32mm miniatures down to 28mm scale. This is calculated by dividing the target eye-level (28mm) by the source eye-level (32mm): 28 ÷ 32 = 0.875.

How tall are 1:48 scale human figures?

A 1:48 scale human figure (175cm real height) measures approximately 36.5mm to the top of the head, or 33.3mm to eye level. This falls between 32mm and 35mm gaming scales.

Does 3D printing filament shrink? Do I need to compensate?

PLA shrinks approximately 0.8-2% as it cools. PETG shrinks 0.2-0.8%. ABS shrinks 0.5-0.8%. For most miniature scaling, this shrinkage is within acceptable tolerance. If precision matters, add 0.5-1% to your calculated scale percentage.
