{{ site.name }} is a free, browser-based STL scale converter for 3D printed miniatures. It converts between 15+ gaming scales with accurate percentages based on verified measurements.

Why I Built This

I'm kitsorix, a 3D printing hobbyist. Every time I converted miniatures between systems, I had to Google the conversion percentage. {{ site.name }} does the math for you — no downloads, no accounts, no ads.

Measurement Method

All measurements use a {{ site.baselineHeightCm }}cm human baseline. Gaming scales (28mm, 32mm, 35mm, 40mm, 75mm) are measured to the eye level at {{ site.eyeLevelCm }}cm. Ratio scales (1:35, 1:48, 1:56, 1:72, 1:100) are measured to the top of the head at {{ site.baselineHeightCm }}cm.

Data Verification

All conversion percentages and scale measurements are verified against multiple community sources including published standards and community measurement databases.

Open Source

The source code is available on GitHub. Contributions, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome.
